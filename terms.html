Service Agreement and Safety Guidelines

MenuQR · Version 1.2

In short

  • MenuQR is only a software tool. Everything about your restaurant and your customers is your own responsibility.
  • You keep your own backups and keep your account safe.
  • We are not responsible for losses, lost data, fake orders, downtime or any dispute. You use the service at your own risk.

1. What this service is

MenuQR gives you a digital menu, QR codes and a way to receive orders. We only provide the software. We do not cook, sell, deliver or collect payment for your food, and we are not part of any sale between you and your customers.

2. Your responsibility

You are fully responsible for:

  • Your menu, prices, pictures, taxes and charges. Check them regularly. Make sure they are correct and that you have the right to use the pictures.
  • Food quality, halal status, allergens, hygiene and any licence or tax your business needs.
  • Preparing, delivering and getting paid for every order, and any complaint, refund or dispute with a customer.
  • The people you give access to. What they do in your account is your responsibility.

3. Keep your account safe

  • Use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else. Never share it, and never send it by message.
  • Sign out on shared or public devices.
  • Give each staff member their own account with only the rights they need. Disable the account when someone leaves.
  • If you think someone else has your password, change it at once and tell the admin.
  • Be careful with messages or calls that ask for your password or login. Nobody from MenuQR will ask for it.

4. Customer information

  • Customer names, phone numbers and addresses may be used only to complete their order.
  • Never sell, share, publish or use them for spam or advertising.
  • Do not leave printed orders or receipts where others can read them. Destroy them when you no longer need them.

5. Orders: check before you act

  • Anyone can send an order. Fake, mistaken or repeated orders can happen, including from bots.
  • A receipt picture can be edited. Before you cook or send a delivery, check the order number in the Orders tab (“Check a receipt”) and compare the items and total.
  • For big, strange or doubtful orders (especially delivery), confirm with the customer by phone first.
  • Switch off an order type or mark the restaurant closed whenever you cannot take orders.

6. Backups

  • Download a backup from the Backup tab regularly (at least once a month, and before big changes).
  • Keep the backup file in a safe place. It contains your business information.
  • Data can be lost for many reasons. We do not promise to recover it. Your own backup is your protection.

7. The service is “as is”

The service is provided as it is and as it is available, without any promise. It can be slow, stop, show mistakes or change at any time. It depends on other services we do not control (for example Cloudflare, GitHub, WhatsApp, your internet and your customers’ phones). Do not depend on it as your only way to take orders.

8. Plans and fees

  • Paid plans are paid in advance and are generally not refunded.
  • When a paid plan ends, Premium features keep working for 3 more days. After that they switch off, but your menu stays available to customers. Free trials end with no extra days.
  • Plans, features and prices can change.

9. Proper use

  • Do not sell anything illegal or harmful, and do not post offensive, false or misleading content.
  • Do not try to break, overload, copy or misuse the service, and do not use bots or tools against it.
  • An account that breaks these guidelines can be suspended or removed at any time, without notice.

10. We are not responsible

To the fullest extent allowed, MenuQR and the people who run it are not responsible or liable for any loss, damage or trouble connected with the service, including:

  • lost sales, lost or damaged data, wrong prices, wrong or missed orders;
  • fake or repeated orders, bots, hacking, a stolen or shared password, or misuse by anyone;
  • downtime, slowness, bugs, or failure of any other service the app depends on;
  • disputes, complaints or claims from customers, staff or anyone else;
  • anything caused by not following these guidelines.

You use MenuQR at your own risk and you are responsible for your own business decisions.

11. Changes

These guidelines can change. When they do, you will be asked to accept the new version the next time you sign in. By signing in and using the service you agree to the version shown here.
